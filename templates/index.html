Frequently Asked Questions

How does the prompt-based extraction process work?

You paste Saariv's Discovery Prompt into your chatbots. It generates the topics and subtopics over which you've had deep chats. You copy that output into Saariv and select only the topics you wish to share. Saariv then generates a second Deep Extraction Prompt for those topics. You paste that into your chatbots, copy the output back into Saariv, and test your avatar in the Playground.

What is the Playground?

The Playground is your private sandbox environment where you can ask your avatar questions, inspect its tone, and verify its accuracy before going live. Only when you're 100% satisfied do you click 'Go Live' to generate your public link.

How does monetization work without upfront payment?

You never pay an upfront setup fee or monthly SaaS subscription. You set your own price per question (e.g. $8, $15, or $50). When a follower or customer pays to ask a question, we take a modest commission cut to cover hosting and AI inference, and the rest is deposited straight to your account.

Can I use this purely as a Living Biography?

Yes! Many creators use Saariv as an interactive Living Biography where friends, family, or community members can come and explore their personal milestones, life transitions, and lessons learned through interactive conversations.
