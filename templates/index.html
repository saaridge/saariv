Frequently Asked Questions

How does Saariv extract my frameworks from chat archives?

All major platforms provide full data archives. Saariv’s ingestion pipeline parses these threads, applies automated PII stripping, and isolates the decision heuristics, evaluation rubrics, and diagnostic patterns that define your advisory style.

How does the 12% revenue share model work?

There are zero upfront or recurring SaaS subscription fees. When a client pays for an asynchronous consultation through your link, Saariv retains 12% to cover LLM inference and infrastructure, and 88% is settled directly to your bank account via Stripe Connect.

Can clients still book my premium 1-on-1 Zoom sessions?

Yes. Saariv acts as an effective top-of-funnel qualifier. Clients who receive initial automated guidance can easily escalate to your full calendar rate for high-touch bespoke engagements.
