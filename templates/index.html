Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Living Biography?

A Living Biography is an interactive, conversational representation of your life journey, decisions, and lessons. Instead of someone reading a static bio or LinkedIn profile, they can have a real conversation with your AI avatar, asking about your specific moves, challenges, and insights.

Why do I need to turn on persistent memory in my chatbot?

Prompt 1 instructs the chatbot to review your past discussions, summaries, and persistent memory across your entire conversation history to extract an accurate taxonomy. Enabling memory ensures the chatbot can search across all previous discussions rather than just a single blank thread.

How does the Playground answer questions about my journey?

When you complete Step 4, Saariv stores your extracted experiences in your private session and indexes each topic. When you ask a question in the Playground, our search engine finds the matching experience in your data and generates an answer strictly grounded in your authentic story.

How does monetization work without upfront payment?

You never pay an upfront setup fee or monthly SaaS subscription. You set your own price per question (e.g. $8, $15, or $50). When a follower or customer pays to ask a question, we take a modest commission cut to cover hosting and AI inference, and the rest is deposited straight to your account.
