Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Saariv extract my persona from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok?

All major LLMs let you export your chat history in one click as JSON or text. Saariv parses these discussions, identifying your recurring mental models, diagnosis questions, tone of voice, and specialized frameworks. It strips out casual chitchat and isolates your advisory DNA.

Is my private information or personal conversations safe?

Absolutely. Your raw data is never exposed publicly or sold to third parties. Our synthesis pipeline applies zero-knowledge sanitization to strip credit card numbers, personal identifiers, passwords, and private notes. You also receive full guardrail controls to set boundaries on what your persona discusses.

How does the pricing work if there's no upfront cost?

We believe in true alignment. You never pay a monthly subscription fee. When a client books or pays for guidance through your Saariv link, we automatically take a modest commission cut (15%), and you receive the remaining 85% directly into your bank account. If you don't earn, we don't earn.

Where do I post my Saariv link?

Anywhere your audience finds you! Most coaches and consultants put it directly in their LinkedIn bio ("Ask my AI twin 24/7 at saariv.com/@myname"), Twitter/X bio, Substack newsletter footer, Linktree, or portfolio website.

Can clients still book my live 1-on-1 calls?

Yes! In fact, Saariv is the ultimate top-of-funnel conversion tool. Clients who get value from your AI persona can click a single button to upgrade and book your full 1-on-1 Zoom session at your standard premium rate.
