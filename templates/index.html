Common Questions

What exactly is a living biography?
A living biography is an AI-powered version of yourself that answers questions using your authentic experiences and unique perspective. Unlike a static bio or resume, it interacts, responds, and shares your knowledge in real-time conversations.
How does the 2-prompt extraction work?
Prompt 1 asks your chatbot to analyze your conversation history and create a taxonomy of topics. Prompt 2 extracts specific lived experiences for each topic. Both are simple copy-paste operations — no technical skills needed.
How do I earn money?
When people ask your living biography questions, you earn credits per interaction. You set your own rates. Credits can be converted to cash or used within the platform. The more valuable your unique perspective, the more you earn.
What about privacy?
Your raw chat logs never leave your device. You control exactly which topics and lived experiences get indexed. No personal identifiers are extracted. You curate what your living biography shares.
Do I need coding skills?
No. If you can copy and paste prompts into ChatGPT, you can create your living biography. The entire process is designed for non-technical users.
