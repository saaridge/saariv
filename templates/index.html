Prompt 1: Taxonomy & Topic Extractor
You are analyzing my full conversation history with you. Go through every conversation you have access to (persistent memory, past chat summaries, titles, excerpts, and this current chat) and build a complete taxonomy of everything I have discussed with you. TASK Extract every Domain, Subdomain, Topic, and Subtopic that appears in my conversations, and return them as a single JSON object. DEFINITIONS - Domain: a broad field (e.g., Software Engineering, Finance, Career, Health). - Subdomain: a specialization inside a domain (e.g., Backend Engineering inside Software Engineering). - Topic: a specific subject I asked about or worked on within a subdomain. - Subtopic: a specific question, concept, tool, decision, or problem inside a topic. RULES 1. Cover everything. Include casual, one-off, and personal topics, not only professional ones. 2. Use only evidence from my conversations. Do not invent topics or infer interests I never discussed. 3. Merge near-duplicate topics, but keep distinct questions as separate subtopics. 4. For each topic, include a short evidence note (what I asked or did) and an approximate date or period if known, otherwise "unknown". 5. Add a "frequency" field per topic: "one-off", "occasional", or "recurring". 6. Add a "confidence" field per topic: "high", "medium", or "low", based on how directly the evidence supports it. 7. If your access to my history is partial, say so in the "coverage_notes" field and list what may be missing. Do not pretend to have seen everything. 8. Do not include any personal identifiers (passwords, API keys, phone numbers, addresses). 9. Output ONLY valid JSON. No markdown, no commentary before or after. OUTPUT FORMAT { "metadata": { "generated_on": "YYYY-MM-DD", "source_scope": "what history you had access to", "coverage_notes": "limitations or gaps", "total_domains": 0, "total_topics": 0 }, "domains": [ { "domain": "Domain name", "subdomains": [ { "subdomain": "Subdomain name", "topics": [ { "topic": "Topic name", "evidence": "One-line description of what I asked or did", "period": "YYYY-MM or range or unknown", "frequency": "one-off | occasional | recurring", "confidence": "high | medium | low", "subtopics": [ "Subtopic 1", "Subtopic 2" ] } ] } ] } ] } Before finalizing, check that no conversation topic is missing, that every topic sits under the correct domain and subdomain, and that the JSON is valid.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Living Biography?

A Living Biography is an interactive, conversational representation of your life journey, decisions, and lessons. Instead of someone reading a static bio or LinkedIn profile, they can have a real conversation with your AI avatar, asking about your specific moves, challenges, and insights.

Why do I need to turn on persistent memory in my chatbot?

Prompt 1 instructs the chatbot to review your past discussions, summaries, and persistent memory across your entire conversation history to extract an accurate taxonomy. Enabling memory ensures the chatbot can search across all previous discussions rather than just a single blank thread.

How does the RAG system in the Playground work?

When you complete Step 4, Saariv stores your extracted experiences in a local session folder and indexes each topic chunk. When you ask a question in the Playground, our RAG retrieval system searches your indexed chunks, extracts the exact matching experience, and generates an answer strictly grounded in your authentic story.
